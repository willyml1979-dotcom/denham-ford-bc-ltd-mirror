Meet the 2026 Ford Bronco Sport Badlands 4x4, a rugged SUV built for drivers who want everyday comfort with real off-road confidence. Finished in striking Carbonized Grey Metallic, this Bronco Sport has a bold look to match its adventurous personality. Its black aluminum wheels, black trim accents, roof rails, skid plates, tow hooks, and distinctive Bronco styling all hint that this SUV is ready for trails, back roads, and weekend escapes. Under the hood, you’ll find a spirited 2.0L EcoBoost turbocharged 4-cylinder engine with 250 horsepower, paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission and Four Wheel Drive. Ford’s Terrain Management System with G.O.A.T. Modes, Trail Control, hill descent control, and the advanced HOSS 2.0 off-road suspension help give this SUV excellent capability when pavement ends.

With features like a full-size spare tire, trailer sway control, and a Class II tow rating, it is designed to handle work, play, and adventure with ease. Inside, the Bronco Sport Badlands offers a smart and comfortable cabin with heated front seats, ActiveX leatherette upholstery, dual-zone climate control, a 13.2-inch primary touchscreen, and a 12.3-inch gauge cluster display. Ford SYNC 4, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, Bluetooth connectivity, SiriusXM capability, and a 5G modem keep you connected wherever you go. Convenience is everywhere, from remote start and hands-free Intelligent Access to multiple USB ports, power outlets, and memory settings. Safety and driver assistance are also a strong point, with adaptive cruise control, blind spot monitoring, lane keeping alert, pre-collision assist with automatic emergency braking, a 360-degree camera, parking sensors, and many airbags.

The 2026 Bronco Sport Badlands 4x4 is a versatile, feature-rich SUV that blends daily practicality with serious off-road attitude.
