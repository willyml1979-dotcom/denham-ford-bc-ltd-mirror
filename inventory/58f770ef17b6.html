Meet the 2026 Ford Bronco Sport Badlands 4x4 with Tech Package, finished in striking Ruby Red Metallic Tinted Clearcoat. This SUV is built for drivers who want everyday comfort with real adventure-ready capability. Under the hood, it packs a 2.0L EcoBoost turbocharged 4-cylinder gas engine with 250 horsepower, paired to an 8-speed automatic transmission and four-wheel drive. With Ford’s Terrain Management System and G.O.A.T. Modes, Trail Control hill descent help, selectable drive settings, and a rear locking differential, it’s ready to take on gravel roads, muddy trails, and city streets with equal confidence. The Badlands trim brings rugged style and serious hardware, including 17-inch black aluminum wheels, all-terrain tires, skid plates, tow hooks, roof rails, and a trailer hitch with sway control.

The exterior looks bold and purposeful with black accents, deep tinted windows, LED lighting, fog lamps, and a roof accent two-tone paint scheme. Safety and visibility are a big priority too, thanks to the 360-degree camera system, front and rear parking sensors, BLIS blind spot monitoring, lane keeping alert, adaptive cruise control, and pre-collision assist with automatic emergency braking. Inside, the Bronco Sport balances durability and comfort with ActiveX leatherette seating, heated front seats, power driver adjustments, dual-zone climate control, and a roomy cabin with flexible rear seats and smart cargo storage. The Tech Package adds a large 13.2-inch touchscreen, a 12.3-inch digital gauge cluster, SYNC 4, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, wireless smartphone connectivity, and a 5G modem for connected convenience.

There are also multiple USB ports, power outlets, remote start, keyless access, and voice-activated controls to make every drive easier. With an EPA-style economy estimate of 11.2 KM city and 8.7 KM highway, this Bronco Sport offers a strong blend of efficiency, technology, and trail capability. If you want a compact SUV that feels premium, practical, and ready for adventure, this one stands out immediately.
