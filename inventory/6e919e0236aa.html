Inside, the cabin offers thoughtful storage, 60-40 folding rear seats, dual USB ports, rear climate ducts, and a digital gauge cluster for clear information at a glance. Exterior highlights include LED headlights, fog lamps, machined aluminum wheels, black body cladding, roof rails, deep-tinted glass, and a roof accent two-tone paint scheme.
