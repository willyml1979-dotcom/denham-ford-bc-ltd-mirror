Meet the 2026 Ford Bronco Sport Big Bend 4x4 with the Black Diamond Package, finished in crisp Oxford White and built for drivers who want everyday comfort with a confident adventurous edge. This compact SUV brings a rugged personality to the road with Four Wheel Drive, a turbocharged 1.5L EcoBoost 3-cylinder engine, and an 8-speed automatic transmission that helps deliver a smooth, responsive drive. With city and highway fuel economy rated at 9.3 KM and 7.8 KM respectively, it’s a smart choice for commuting, weekend escapes, and everything in between. The Bronco Sport stands out with its Black Diamond Package touches and bold exterior details like black cladding, black door handles, roof rails, LED lighting, and 17-inch grey aluminum wheels. Inside, the cabin is designed to feel practical and modern, offering heated front seats, cloth upholstery, a 13.2-inch primary touchscreen, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, SYNC 4, Bluetooth audio streaming, and a 6-speaker sound system.

Comfort and convenience are well covered with automatic climate control, remote start, keyless entry, push-button start, multiple USB ports, and plenty of storage solutions throughout the cabin. Safety and driver assistance features are another highlight. You’ll find Ford’s Co-Pilot-style support systems including Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop-and-Go, Blind Spot Information System, Lane Keeping Alert, Pre-Collision Assist with Automatic Emergency Braking, Rear Cross-Traffic Braking, rear parking sensors, a rearview camera, and a full suite of airbags. The Terrain Management System with G.O.A.T. Modes, selectable terrain settings, hill start assist, and the HOSS 1.0 off-road suspension help give this SUV the confidence to handle rougher trails when the pavement ends. If you’re looking for an SUV that blends style, technology, safety, and capability in a friendly, ready-for-anything package, this Bronco Sport Big Bend is a great fit.
