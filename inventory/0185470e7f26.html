Meet the 2026 Ford F-150 Lariat, a tough and polished truck that blends everyday comfort with serious capability. Finished in Agate Black Metallic, this 4WD SuperCrew with a 5.5-foot box brings a confident look that’s matched by the strength under the hood. Its EcoBoost 3.5L V6 engine delivers 382 horsepower, paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission for smooth, responsive driving whether you’re heading to work, towing a trailer, or taking the long way home. Inside, the F-150 Lariat feels more like a premium lounge than a traditional pickup. You’ll find heated and ventilated front seats, ActiveX leatherette upholstery, dual-zone automatic climate control, a heated steering wheel, memory settings, and a 12-inch primary display that keeps important information right where you want it.

The spacious SuperCrew cabin includes plenty of smart storage, lots of USB ports, and convenient features like push-button start, remote start, and hands-free access. Technology is a major highlight here. SYNC 4 with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, connected navigation, a 5G modem, wireless device connectivity, and a Bang & Olufsen sound system help make every drive easier and more enjoyable. Safety and driver assistance are also well covered with Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop-and-Go, BLIS blind spot monitoring, Lane Keeping Alert, Pre-Collision Assist with Pedestrian Detection, a 360-degree camera, Reverse Brake Assist, and trailer-focused tools like Pro Trailer Backup Assist and Pro Trailer Hitch Assist. Built to work hard, this F-150 includes a Class IV tow rating, trailer sway control, a trailer hitch and wiring harness, four-wheel drive, all-terrain tires, running boards, and a durable aluminum body.

From weekday commuting to weekend hauling, this Lariat is ready for almost anything with style, comfort, and confidence.
