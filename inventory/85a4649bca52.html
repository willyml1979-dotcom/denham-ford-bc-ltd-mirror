The Black Appearance Package gives it extra attitude with black grille, black mirrors, black door handles, and black side window trim for a bold, modern look. Inside, the F-150 offers a spacious and practical cabin with seating for the crew, cloth upholstery, 40/20/40 split front bench, and 60/40 folding rear seats. Comfort and convenience features include a 12-inch touchscreen, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, SYNC 4 voice controls, 6 USB ports, remote start, keyless entry, cruise control, and a 5G modem with Ford Connectivity Package mobile hotspot access. There’s also an integrated 7-speaker audio system with SiriusXM 360L, so every drive feels more enjoyable. Safety and confidence come standard with BLIS blind spot monitoring, lane keeping alert, pre-collision assist with pedestrian detection, reverse camera, reverse sensing system, cross-traffic alert with reverse brake assist, and a full suite of airbags and stability technologies.

Add in LED headlights, fog lights, power mirrors, and smart utility features like integrated bed storage, bed-rail protectors, and a tailgate with power lock, and you have a truck that’s as capable as it is polished.
