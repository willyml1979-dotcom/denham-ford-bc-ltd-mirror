Meet the 2026 Ford Bronco Big Bend 4x4 with the Black Diamond Package, a rugged SUV built for drivers who want everyday comfort with real trail-ready confidence. Finished in eye-catching Ruby Red Metallic Tinted Clearcoat, this 4-door 4x4 stands out with bold black accents, black fender flares, black grille, black bumpers, and deep-tinted windows that give it a tough, adventurous look from every angle. Under the hood, the Bronco uses a 2.3L EcoBoost I-4 turbocharged gas engine with 275 horsepower, paired to a 7-speed manual transmission and part-time four-wheel drive. Ford’s Terrain Management System with G.O.A.T. Modes, Hill Descent Control, HOSS 1.0 off-road suspension, and a two-speed transfer case help make rough roads, muddy paths, and steep grades feel far more manageable.

It also comes with front tow hooks, a rear tow hook, skid-ready confidence, and a full-size spare tire mounted outside for easy access. Inside, this Bronco is thoughtfully equipped with heated front seats, dual-zone climate control, a 12-inch touchscreen, SYNC 4 with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, connected navigation, and wireless device connectivity. A leather steering wheel, selectable ambient lighting, and cloth seating give the cabin a comfortable yet durable feel. Practical touches like removable rear windshield, retractable soft top, cargo tie-downs, concealed storage, and folding rear seats make it easy to load gear for weekend escapes or daily errands. Safety and convenience are also well covered, with BLIS blind spot monitoring, pre-collision assist with pedestrian detection, lane-keeping system, automatic emergency braking, rear camera, reverse sensing system, and Ford Co-Pilot360 features.

Add in remote start, intelligent access, push-button start, over-the-air updates, and a vehicle tracker, and this Bronco is ready for both modern life and off-road fun. It’s a smart, capable SUV with plenty of personality.
