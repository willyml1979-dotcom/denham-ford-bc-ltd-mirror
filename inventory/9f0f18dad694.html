Meet the 2026 Ford Explorer ST AWD, a bold and capable SUV finished in striking Star White Met Tri-coat. This Explorer brings together everyday practicality, sporty character, and a long list of premium features that make every drive feel special. Whether you are commuting through town, heading out on a family road trip, or towing gear for the weekend, this SUV is ready for it all. Inside, the cabin feels upscale and thoughtfully designed.
