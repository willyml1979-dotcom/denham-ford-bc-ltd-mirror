Introducing the 2026 Ford Expedition Platinum Max 4x4, a full-size SUV designed to deliver commanding capability, premium comfort, and advanced technology in one sophisticated package. Finished in Agate Black Metallic, this Expedition presents a strong, refined presence with a black grille accented in chrome, LED lighting, running boards, roof rails, and 20 x 8.5-inch machined aluminum wheels that reinforce its upscale character. Its powerful 3.5L EcoBoost V6 engine produces 440 horsepower and pairs with a 10-speed automatic transmission and Four Wheel Drive for confident performance in a wide range of road and weather conditions. Inside, the Platinum Max offers a highly appointed cabin with leather seating, heated and ventilated front seats, heated rear seats, wood and metal-look interior accents, a heated steering wheel, and power-adjustable first- and second-row seating.

The spacious three-row layout includes second-row bucket seats, power-reclining third-row seats, and versatile cargo solutions, making it ideal for family travel or long-distance adventure. Convenience features abound, including Intelligent Access with hands-free access, push-button start, remote start, power liftgate, memory settings, tri-zone climate control, and multiple USB ports and power outlets throughout the cabin. Technology is equally impressive, highlighted by a 13.2-inch primary touchscreen, full digital instrumentation, integrated navigation with voice activation, Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, wireless connectivity, Ford Connect 5G hotspot capability, and a premium B&O Sound System by Bang & Olufsen. Safety and driver assistance features include Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop-and-Go, Lane-Keeping System, BLIS, Pre-Collision Assist with Automatic Emergency Braking, 360-degree camera, parking sensors, traffic sign recognition, and a suite of airbags and stability systems. With a Class IV tow rating, trailer sway control, trailer brake controller, and automated trailer hitching assist, this Expedition is built for serious hauling as well as daily refinement.

Delivering an estimated 14.7 KM city and 10.6 KM highway fuel economy, the 2026 Ford Expedition Platinum Max combines strength, luxury, and versatility in a flagship SUV.
