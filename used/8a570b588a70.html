Meet this 2025 Ford Explorer ST AWD, a bold and athletic SUV finished in eye-catching Vapor Blue Metallic. With 55,296 KM on the odometer, it still brings plenty of adventure-ready life to the road. The ST trim gives this Explorer a sporty personality from every angle, with 21-inch grey aluminum wheels, red-painted brake calipers, dual stainless steel exhaust, black exterior accents, roof rails, a rear lip spoiler, and a muscular stance. Inside, the cabin feels refined and driver-focused with heated, ventilated, and massaging front seats, heated rear seats, second-row captain’s chairs, and a third-row bench for extra flexibility.

You’ll also find leatherette and suede-style upholstery, ambient lighting, a panoramic sunroof, a heated steering wheel, memory settings, and three-zone-style rear climate comfort. Technology is a major highlight here. The Explorer features a 13.2-inch primary touchscreen, integrated navigation, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, FordPass Connect 5G hotspot capability, a 12.3-inch gauge cluster display, wireless charging, and a premium Bang & Olufsen sound system with 14 speakers. Convenience is excellent too, thanks to a power liftgate, remote start, hands-free access, adaptive cruise control with stop-and-go, 360-degree camera, parking sensors, and a host of smart driver aids including BLIS, lane-keeping, traffic sign recognition, and evasive steering assist. Safety and practicality are well covered with seven airbags, advanced braking systems, trailer sway control, trailer hitch and wiring, and a Class III tow rating.
