Introducing the 2025 Ford Explorer Active AWD, a versatile SUV finished in striking Star White Metallic Tri-Coat and engineered to deliver confident capability, modern technology, and everyday comfort. Powered by the efficient yet responsive EcoBoost 2.3L I-4 turbocharged engine producing 300 horsepower, this Explorer pairs its strong gas performance with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission and Four Wheel Drive for secure traction in changing road conditions. With an estimated 20 KM city and 27 KM highway efficiency, it balances power and practicality with ease. Inside, the cabin is thoughtfully designed for families and active lifestyles. Seating for up to seven is supported by a third-row bench, 35-30-35 folding second-row seats, heated front seats, and a power-adjustable driver’s seat with lumbar support.

Multiple USB ports, 12V outlets, and 10 beverage holders help keep everyone connected and organized. Safety and confidence are central to the Explorer’s design. Standard features include BLIS blind spot monitoring, Lane Keeping Alert, Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop-and-Go, Pre-Collision Assist with Pedestrian Detection, Rear Cross-Traffic Braking, Reverse Sensing System, a rearview camera, and Ford’s Safety Canopy airbag system. Additional driver aids such as Hill Descent Control, Hill Start Assist, Driver Alert, and Evasive Steering Assist further enhance peace of mind. Utility is equally impressive, with a power liftgate, roof rails, Class III tow rating, trailer hitch, and trailer sway control.

Riding on 18-inch silver aluminum wheels and dressed in black exterior accents, this Explorer blends rugged style with modern sophistication. With only 9,296 KM, it offers exceptional value in a well-equipped, low-mileage SUV.
