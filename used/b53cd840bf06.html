Meet the 2025 Ford Escape Platinum AWD, a stylish and smart SUV finished in Carbonized Grey Metallic. This Escape brings together confident design, advanced technology, and everyday versatility in a package that feels both refined and ready for adventure. With All Wheel Drive and a 2.0L EcoBoost I-4 engine producing 250 horsepower, it offers responsive performance, smooth automatic CVT operation, and the added traction of selectable terrain modes for changing road conditions. Inside, the cabin is thoughtfully equipped for comfort and convenience. Heated front seats, a heated steering wheel, dual-zone automatic climate control, memory settings, and ActiveX leatherette seating create a premium atmosphere. The driver enjoys an 8-way power seat with lumbar support, while the passenger seat also offers power adjustment.

A large 13.2-inch primary touchscreen and a 12.3-inch digital gauge cluster help keep important information front and center. SYNC 4 with Enhanced Voice Recognition, built-in virtual assistant support, integrated navigation, wireless mirroring, Bluetooth streaming, and a 4G hotspot make staying connected simple. This Escape is also well prepared for family life and daily hauling. The power liftgate, 60/40 folding rear seats, cargo concealment features, roof rails, and generous storage bins make loading gear easy. Safety and driver-assist technology is a major highlight, with BLIS blind spot monitoring, lane keeping alert, adaptive cruise control with stop-and-go, pre-collision assist with pedestrian detection, rear cross-traffic braking, parking sensors, and a high-resolution rear camera. From its LED lighting and machined aluminum wheels to its hands-free access, remote start, and over-the-air updates, this 2025 Escape Platinum AWD blends practicality with upscale appeal.

With impressive fuel economy for an SUV and a long list of comfort, safety, and connectivity features, it’s a well-rounded choice for drivers who want efficiency without giving up premium feel.
